 Next week is Monday 28 Sep through Sunday 4 Oct, prepped today (Sunday 27 Sep). One person, breakfast and
 lunch only. Same bowl all week so Sunday is one cook, not seven recipes.

 Skipped on purpose: uttapam, paratha, samosa, daily scrambled eggs, dried peas, anything fried. Those do not
  survive a batch and they blow the Sunday budget.

 Sunday cook, about 60–70 minutes

 1. Boil 12 eggs. Leave them unpeeled.
 2. Cook 6 rice portions. Cool fast. Fridge 4, freezer 2 (with the Friday and Saturday paneer).
 3. Butter paneer, 4 portions. Fast path: paneer cubes in a jar of butter masala, simmer 15 minutes. Fridge 2
  (Wed–Thu), freezer 2 (pull Friday morning and Saturday morning).
 4. Bake 2 salmon portions, not fried. About 12–15 minutes at 200°C. Fridge for Mon–Tue only.
 5. Four overnight-oat jars.
 6. Buy Greek yogurt, muffins or bread, and a bag of frozen mixed veg (peppers, mushrooms, squash, zucchini,
 broccoli, green beans, carrots). Do not chop or cook the veg.

 No chickpeas, tofu, noodles, or black-eyed peas this week. Fewer items, less prep. Frozen veg goes into the
 lunch box at reheat time so it does not turn to mush on Sunday.
